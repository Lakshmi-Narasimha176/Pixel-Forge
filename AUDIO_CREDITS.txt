Included Packs:
• Impact Sounds:
  - Surface footsteps: Concrete, Wood, Grass, Dirt/Gravel, Carpet
  - Body impacts: Punch, Soft impacts (jump landing, damage)
  - Environmental impacts: Wood, Metal, Glass, Plate, Tin
• RPG Audio:
  - Footsteps: Concrete steps (footstep00 - footstep09)
  - Doors & Architecture: Door opening (1-2), Door closing (1-4), Structural creaks (1-3)
  - Interactive items: Metal latches, clicks, book opening, closing, page turns
  - Weapons & Melee: Knife drawing, knife slicing, axe chopping
• Interface & UI Audio:
  - UI clicks, button presses, confirmation tones, cancel/back sounds, error chimes, switches
• Sci-Fi & Digital Audio:
  - Atmospheric machine hums, computer processing chirps, high frequency electrical tones
