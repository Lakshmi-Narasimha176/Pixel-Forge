# The Last Revenge

A first-person survival horror game in the Hollis family house, built from the Pixel-Forge GLB assets and Godot 4.

## Play

Open `project.godot` in **Godot 4.7.2** (the version used for validation) and run the project. Run `git lfs pull` first so the large GLB assets are available. The game begins in the underground tunnel. Follow the lanterns to the dining room entrance, switch on the outside main breaker, search the house, and escape through the tunnel with the Devil's Bible.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD | Move |
| Mouse | Look |
| Shift | Sprint (noisy) |
| C | Crouch (quiet) |
| F | Toggle flashlight |
| E | Interact, collect items, flip switches, use doors |
| R | Use the walkie-talkie to contact Ada |
| Q | Use one collected blood bag to restore health |
| Left mouse or T | Swing the bloody baseball bat |
| Enter | Start, resume, close a memory, or retry |
| Esc | Pause or close a memory |
| G | Open the asset gallery from the menu |

## Story and missions

Abel Crane is Nora's father and the Butcher. Mira, his wife, haunts the horror corridor. Ada, Nora's younger sister, is the blood-stained ghost in the vintage living room. The translucent Horror Girl, Lily, holds the key to the slaughterhouse. The porcelain doll cries, the street rat bites, and the Butcher hunts by sight and sound.

The required route is: restore power, enter the dining room, find the bloody bat and Ada's radio, defeat Lily **while she is visible**, collect Father's dropped key, unlock the slaughterhouse, take the Devil's Bible, and return to the tunnel. The walkie-talkie gives contextual hints with R. Blood bags are stored until Q is pressed; raw meat and batteries provide smaller recovery.

Optional missions include freeing Ada and Mira with the bat, finding Mira's corridor key, stopping the biting rat, restoring power after a short circuit, and recovering four readable family memories. Finding Pearl's crayon drawing lets you comfort the crying doll. Completing the memories, Ada, Mira, and Pearl missions unlocks the **Family Restored** true ending.

The whole-house main breaker starts off. Every power change can manifest Lily nearby. Room switches control their own lights; turning a room light back on can short the entire house and cut the fan and ceiling lights. The fan creaks, the wall clock ticks, Pearl cries, the rat squeaks before biting, and recorded ambience, footsteps, impacts, ghost sounds, and radio static reinforce the scares.

The slaughterhouse includes a meat-hook rail, hanging pigs, blood-splashed walls, and floor blood pools. A wooden toilet sits in the bathroom, with a vintage parlor for Ada and a haunted corridor for Mira. Interaction and bat attacks use wall-occlusion checks, so locked progression cannot be bypassed through walls.
